Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

lukerebarchik.com is the professional website of Luke Rebarchik. It has no forms, accounts or comments. Apart from what its web host needs to deliver the pages, the only information collected about visitors is what Google Analytics records, as described below.

Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and clicks on links to other websites, chiefly the links to Luke Rebarchik's LinkedIn profile. For each visit, Google Analytics records:

  • the pages viewed, their titles and the previous page;
  • clicks on links that lead to other websites, and whether a visitor scrolled most of the way (90%) down a page;
  • approximate location (city, region and country), which Google derives from the visitor's IP address;
  • the browser, operating system, device type, screen resolution and language.

Google states that Google Analytics discards raw IP addresses after use and never associates them with user identifiers. Google signals, which adds demographic and interest data from signed-in Google accounts, is turned off for this site.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_ followed by an identifier, to tell visitors and sessions apart. On this site they expire at most two months after the most recent visit; some browsers delete them sooner.

If a visitor uses the light and dark switch, that choice is saved in their own browser (local storage) and is never sent anywhere.

How long data is kept

Google Analytics deletes user-level and event-level data for this site after two months. Totals in Google's standard aggregated reports, such as the number of visits in a month, are not affected by that setting and are kept longer.

Global Privacy Control and other choices

If a browser sends Global Privacy Control, this site does not load Google Analytics at all: no script, no cookies and no data sent to Google. Brave and DuckDuckGo send this signal by default, and Firefox offers it as a setting.

Visitors can also block cookies or scripts in their browser, use a content blocker, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. The site is hosted on Microsoft Azure, which, like any web host, receives each visitor's IP address and request details in order to deliver the pages.
